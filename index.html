What this run is doing

Each project is the upstream C++ sources, parsed and interpreted in the browser. Pac-Man Clock publishes a frame at SwapOnVSync. Pong Clock, Matrix Clock, Tetris Clock, Plasma Clock, Snake Clock, Mario Clock, Game of Life, Dance Monkey, and Rexie draw on the matrix itself and publish a frame at usleep. GPIO, the Adafruit HAT, and syslog are host stubs.

Driver brightness is whatever the program sets. Pac-Man Clock uses 50. Pong Clock, Matrix Clock, Tetris Clock, Plasma Clock, Snake Clock, Mario Clock, Game of Life, Dance Monkey, and Rexie leave it at 100. Snake Clock paints its body at 80 and its clock dimmer than that. Mario Clock multiplies every channel by 0.5 before SetPixel. Game of Life scales each channel by 0.5. Dance Monkey and Rexie scale the sprite by 0.2 in the day and 0.1 at night. The Screen slider is separate. 0 shows the raw SetPixel numbers. Above 0 the brightest channel is lifted (100 is the sRGB step, 140 the default, 160 the top) and the other channels follow the panel CIE1931 curve, so a pastel sprite keeps its hue. An LED at 0,0,0 is black, with no centre dot. ?brightness= overrides the saved choice.

Open a project directly with ?project=pacmanclock, pongclock, matrixclock, tetrisclock, plasmaclock, snakeclock, marioclock, gameoflifegame, dancemonkey, or rexie.
