What this run is doing

The page parses pacmanclock.cc and digit.cc, then interprets the subset those files use: SetPixel, the 3×5 digit tables, maze generation, pellet rules, and the 80 ms Pac-Man step. SwapOnVSync publishes one frame. GPIO, the Adafruit HAT, and syslog are host stubs.

Driver brightness in the source is 50. The preview shows the raw SetPixel colours. The physical panel dims those in the row scan.
